eCommerce product consulting

What makes someone buy? What makes a seller list? Start there. I help teams turn those questions into a clear product direction, a focused experiment, and something customers can actually use.

Talk about what you need ↗

Find the problem worth solving

A catalog is only one part of a shopping experience. Customers need to find relevant options, understand the differences, and feel ready to choose. Sellers need a reason to participate and an easier way to get their products online. We look at the journey, the available data, and the friction before deciding what to build.

Turn customer insight into product decisions

The work can include customer discovery, journey mapping, a review of the buying or selling flow, and an experiment roadmap. Together, we define the core proposition, decide what belongs in the first release, and agree what evidence would justify the next step.

Take the idea into a working experience

I connect product direction with design and full-stack development. That can mean testing a new discovery experience, prototyping a simpler listing flow, or building an MVP to explore a different way to shop. The scope follows the problem you need to solve.

Can you help improve an existing eCommerce experience?

Yes. We can start with a specific part of the journey—discovery, comparison, product understanding or seller listing—and work through the customer problem, possible changes and a focused way to test them.

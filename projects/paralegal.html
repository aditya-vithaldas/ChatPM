Legal operations / Agentic workflows / Concept outline

Agentic engineering for paralegal firms

Connecting matter context, document work, and deliberate human review.

Try the concept ↓

01 / The question

How might a workspace reduce the effort of finding and reviewing matter information without hiding the source or bypassing human judgment?

The following is an initial concept hypothesis, not a report of completed research or measured results.

02 / Thought process

The why.

Start with the handoffs: where people find documents, gather context, and check work before it moves forward.

The what.

A matter workspace with document context, source references, and an explicit review state.

The how.

Prototype a small review loop using clearly fictional sample documents. Keep the reviewer in control of the final status.

03 / Try the interaction

A small part of the experience.

Choose a sample document, inspect its context, and mark it reviewed. Switch documents to see the progress carry across the matter.

04 / Design rationale

The decision behind the interaction.

Keep the source beside the summary. A reviewer should be able to inspect context before marking anything reviewed.

05 / What to learn next

From hypothesis to evidence.

Observe whether reviewers can find the source and understand what still needs attention. Test the handoff before adding automation.

04 · Product thesis

Make the person stronger.
Make the work easier.
Make the network smarter.

Three bets. They reinforce each other: capability augmentation creates better work signals; invisible coordination creates better outcome data; better data makes matching much sharper.

01 · SUPERHUMAN TALENT

Take someone really good. Then give them leverage.

Keep the specialist core. Add virtual design, engineering, content, research and ops counterparts around the gaps.

02 · Color

The sunset gradient

Five stops, always in this order, always at 45°. Use it for backgrounds, rings, and one highlighted word per frame. Text on the gradient is always white.

Linear · primary

Backgrounds, title cards, gradient text. Angle 45°, yellow bottom-left to blue top-right.

Radial · accent

Avatars, app-icon style tiles, end cards. Glow origin sits just below the bottom-left.

06 · Rules

Keep it consistent

Do
  • Keep one gradient per frame, at 45°, stops in order.
  • Put white text on the gradient; dark text only on neutral ground.
  • Highlight one word per title with gradient text on neutral frames.
  • Use the script face for a short kicker above the title.
  • Land every animation on a round resting state within 600 ms.
  • Burn in captions in Medium 500, max two lines, inside the safe zone.
Don't
  • Use the Instagram logo, glyph or wordmark: this is a look, not their brand.
  • Reverse, rotate, or recolor the gradient stops.
  • Set body text in the script face or in all caps.
  • Use action blue and heart red as decoration.
  • Place text in the top 220 px or bottom 420 px of a Reel.
  • Stack more than two motions on one element at the same time.

## Sound direction

- Soft modern product-film instrumental; no vocals.
- Calm observation at the opening, analytical focus through the investigation, warm confidence at the close.
- Scene changes are carried by restrained musical lifts, not electronic pings or notification sounds.
- Keep the bed sparse enough for continuous narration and three short human voice moments.
- Narration should feel observant and assured, with emotion carried by phrasing, breath and pace rather than theatrical intensity.
